# Digital Forensics — Case Studies

Case studies from a graduate digital forensics course, covering disk, mobile, and cloud evidence acquisition and analysis, plus two written analyses on forensics program strategy. Tool screenshots included where they demonstrate a specific finding.

- [Cross-File-System Forensic Examination](Cross_File_System_Forensic_Examination.pdf) — Comparative analysis of FAT32, NTFS, and HFS+ disk images using FTK Imager, covering drive geometry, file-system fingerprinting by hex signature, and how each system handles deleted data.

- [Mobile Device Forensic Examination](Mobile_Device_Forensic_Examination.pdf) — Recovery and verification of evidence from a mobile handset's storage using Autopsy and FTK Imager, including file carving, EXIF-based device attribution, and the practical limits of carving-based recovery.

- [Cloud Storage Forensic Examination](Cloud_Storage_Forensic_Examination.pdf) — Reconstructing account activity from local sync artifacts across Dropbox, Google Drive, and OneDrive, including direct SQLite database queries, encoded-log limitations, and reconciling a sync log against what's actually on disk.

- [Digital Forensics Lab Business Case](Digital_Forensics_Lab_Business_Case.pdf) — Written framework for building the business case for an in-house digital forensics lab, covering feasibility, cost-benefit analysis, accreditation, and CAPEX/OPEX budget structure.

- [Cloud Evidence Acquisition — Client Intake Memo](Cloud_Evidence_Acquisition_Client_Intake_Memo.pdf) — Pre-engagement technical and legal questions to resolve with a client before accepting a cloud data acquisition case, covering service models, encryption/key control, jurisdiction, and chain of custody.
