# Botium Toys — Security Risk Assessment & Controls Audit

Case study assessing a fictional retail/e-commerce company's security 
posture. Conducted a risk assessment aligned to the NIST Cybersecurity 
Framework, then evaluated existing controls and compliance against 
PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC 2 requirements.

- [Risk Assessment Report](Botium_Toys_Risk_Assessment.pdf)
- [Controls & Compliance Checklist](Botium_Toys_Controls_Compliance_Assessment.pdf)

**Key finding:** Risk score of 8/10, driven primarily by the absence 
of encryption on stored/transmitted cardholder data and the lack of 
least privilege / separation of duties across employee access.
